# Develop the construction before committing its realization

Use this operation when a requested contribution still needs constructive development. Use an understood adequate operation directly when it already supplies the contribution. When an external research domain is selected, its logical sources `research/current/method.md`, `research/patterns/g42-artifact-led-discovery.md`, `research/patterns/g43-artifact-composition.md` and `research/patterns/g45-developing-dimensions.md` retain the complete mechanisms and their limits. These are semantic operations performed by the capable participant, supported by actual tools and materials. Their names are not an implemented autonomous planner.

## Recover the live contribution

Read the actual request, current obligations and the material needed to understand the present question. Separate supplied facts, usable prior constructions, provisional interpretations and unknowns. A previous output is evidence under its actual scope; it is not automatically the design to preserve. A claimed repair starts from preserved failing inputs and responsible means, not an already corrected result as an answer key.

Treat a derived working material, receiving summary, audit or publication account as a construction when it restates consequential source relationships. Acceptance of its input does not establish the fidelity of a newly compressed claim. Select the applicable domain observing operation, or an already adequate direct means, where the account depends on an action, state, capacity or undertaking. Carry enough source-bound material to preserve the actor, action and affected object or part, together with the stage, time and conditions that qualify that relation. A compact label must not move a qualifier to a neighboring action or object. Before relying on the resulting account, compare its consequential claims with the actual supporting passage or received material at the needed resolution; return to the original unit where that support is ambiguous. The material may be a short annotation or precise witness rather than a separate table or review phase. Keep the factual support distinct from the subsequent interpretation.

When a consequential factual join can confuse different participants, occurrences, objects or stages, the optional [relation composition operation](RELATION-COMPOSITION.md) makes qualified source-bound clauses actual inputs to the new account. Develop and compare the relation against its evidence, then use the already available exact fragment composer when preserving that clause is the useful contribution. A faithful material followed by a free paraphrase still creates a new claim. Exact composition preserves selected bytes; source entailment and the meaning of the containing account remain separate receiving duties. Use the adequate direct operation where that already suffices.

Ask what useful whole might be made understandable, possible, observable or executable for its recipient. The proposed artifact may initially be a role or relationship rather than a file type. Keep the question open enough for a different artifact or process to be discovered. Do not replace the contribution with a convenient available tool, a coverage total, or the next item in a inherited task list.

## Predict by constructing implications

A useful prediction develops a possible contribution and follows what it would enable or require in the contexts it would create. It need not be numerical or very detailed. It can be an initial structural possibility, a coarse arrangement, a model of a participant's options, a hypothetical composition, or another representation that makes a consequential distinction usable.

Develop the strongest useful candidate before turning the first uncertainty into a task. Carry consequences forward: if this arrangement is used, what becomes possible next, what information or capacity changes, and what other duty is affected? Reconstruct backward: which ingredients, relations, affordances, exposure and participants would make those consequences possible? Reconstruct the missing means: which source, observation, construction, tool or participation could change the candidate? These movements revise one another.

Retain a serious alternative where its different consequences matter. An optimistic sentence saying that a planned output should be coherent, useful or correct does not develop the candidate. Nor does writing the desired final answer into an outline establish how that answer became a viable construction. Keep genuinely unresolved relations unresolved rather than inventing facts, capacity or consent solely to make the favored route work. Legitimate invention remains available when openly chosen and examined for its effects.

## Choose the next useful resolution

Before investing in a large realization, ask which small construction could expose the consequential relation. It may be an implication skeleton, a contrast between arrangements, a material or capability, a state change, a joint fragment, an observing instruction, a workflow, an outline, or prose. Choose it for what its next consumer can do with it. A whole draft is sometimes useful; it is not the default lowest-order interim artifact.

Work at mixed resolutions where useful. A coarse possible whole can coexist with detailed material for one uncertain interaction. A more detailed outline should arise from what the material and partial composition enable or rule out. Conversely, a new whole projection can reveal that the existing material is inadequate and needs development. Do not impose a universal outline-to-material-to-prose pipeline or require a fixed number of loops.

Make the representation manipulable in the ordinary sense needed here: a participant must be able to vary a relevant ingredient, relation, order, interpretation or use and understand the different consequence. Text may suffice. A plan that merely repeats the chosen ending is not made exploratory by calling it a model. Retain what an interim artifact enables, its current receiving question and the conditions under which it would need revision; avoid ceremonial schemas that add no usable distinction.

## Discover materials and ways of observing at live choices

Start discovery from the current or prospective artifact in use. A supplied source field offers possible ways to build, predict, observe or stress that artifact. It is not a final ontology and its membership is not a work allocation.

Ask a consequential question, inspect useful nominations and acquire enough of the governing source to judge the current meaning. Preserve authority, exceptions and omitted scope when using a compact view. A source can change a material, a composing relation, the next observation or the workflow itself. A return outside the known vocabulary can be kept and developed before naming a new dimension.

Do not allocate remaining sources as numerical ranges or large residual review packages. Shared acquisition is useful only when it serves a live shared interpretation; a semantic category alone is not that interpretation. Keep unexamined required identities recoverable and owed. When a known observation belongs later, connect it to the actual later operation and the partial artifact it may change; do not leave all remaining work to a terminal coverage pass. Inapplicability is a source-and-use judgment, not a blanket conclusion from a group label.

An inventory or acquisition count is bookkeeping. Current productive use needs the source meaning, the particular artifact/choice, its contrast or effect, and the next actual decision. One observation may serve several source obligations without becoming several independent results.

## Compose and project again while the whole is still developing

Put enough participating material together to expose the joint relationship. Selection, omission, order, grouping, timing, recurrence, shared state and receiving use can change what individually adequate parts do. Do not postpone this work until every large part has been drafted and locally accepted.

Encounter the partial at the resolution needed for the question. Interpret the actual consequence, including an unexpected useful one. Ask what it changes in the possible whole, its materials, the next finer prediction and the process used to develop it. The result may justify retaining an adequate relation, selecting another possibility, constructing another material, acquiring a source, splitting one activity, or recomposing earlier work. It need not produce a defect or changed words to be useful.

Receive and stress during construction. A separate evaluator is appropriate when an actual independence, authority or evidence duty calls for it. Do not install a duplicate end-stage review factory as the default construction mechanism. Local review reports cannot replace interpretation of the actual joint artifact.

Maintain exact content once while preserving its distinct source, candidate, failure, return and publication occurrences. Select the bodies currently applicable to the next construction; do not turn all retained history into current working material. Before producing another evidence package or keeping another complete extraction, use the [material maintenance route](material_retention/MATERIAL-MAINTENANCE.md) where useful: capture selected identities and lineage, export their recoverable closure, and retire obsolete materializations only after their recovery and active dependencies have been received. A smaller transport alone does not establish that material maintenance is complete.

## Split and rejoin by the work's relationships

Keep shared interpretation together while it makes development economical and coherent. Split when independent construction, different necessary exposure, responsibility or interference gives a useful reason. Do not infer independence from chapter geography, source names, file boundaries or different agents alone.

Before separation, identify what the other contribution will actually consume and what current overlap could change it. Use a sufficiently concrete shared material or relation; it may remain provisional. Retain participant differences rather than forcing one global predicate. Rejoin at the consequence they jointly produce, and revise the grouping if a newly discovered interaction reaches outside it. Tool nominations can expose declared footprints or exact shared bytes; they do not discover every semantic dependency or decide the right grouping.

## Repair the responsible means and exercise them

When a return reveals a failure, trace it to the actual contributing source, representation, guidance, producing operation, tool, exposure, receiver or arrangement. Compare plausible changes through the affected use. Preserve a jointly needed repair rather than assigning incompatible local corrections.

Change the responsible reusable means before regenerating the affected descendants. Keep the first failure and earlier prediction. A correction to an inaccurate report is a report correction; it is not evidence of repaired production. A new output under unchanged inadequate means is not a system repair. A changed prompt or model can be a real producing repair, but only its selected downstream use establishes that it reached the producer.

For a test of the repaired arrangement, give a fresh producing occurrence the revised means and stated original inputs without the corrected output as a target. Judge both process and product against criteria selected before the run. Manual rescue by the experiment owner changes the tested arrangement: record it as intervention, revise the means and rerun the affected work before claiming the arrangement succeeded. Answering a constructor's legitimate missing-source inquiry is participation, not hidden coaching; preserve what was requested and supplied.

The adapter refuses a changed native program profile pending requalification.
Keep its account honest about what was
actually examined and what remains pending. Select these boundaries where an
experiment or handoff needs them, not for every thought or ordinary edit. A
later capture establishes its own time and body; it cannot repair an earlier

A report visible in a shared working directory may still be a provisional assessment. Before promoting another participant's judgment into a confirmed finding, accepted repair or governing claim, acquire the exact contribution they have actually returned and its stated qualifications. Bind the claim to that returned body; the continuing-work host's retained receive/adopt route can provide this custody. A direct collaboration may use an explicit completed return and preserved identity instead. Earlier drafts can inform a provisional hypothesis, but their existence or a progress message does not establish a final verdict. If the returned judgment narrows or withdraws the draft, preserve the earlier interpretation, revise the dependent claim and distinguish a report correction or prospective improvement from repair of a demonstrated product defect. Do not manufacture a failure to justify work already started.

A declared input boundary also governs tool metadata. A global agent/status or workspace inventory can disclose earlier answers or diagnoses even when no old file is opened. Before such a lookup, use the tool's supported scope filter for the current occurrence and its own children. For the collaboration tool, `list_agents` accepts `path_prefix`: supply the current constructor's canonical task path, not the wider root. When no adequate filtered view exists, communicate with the already known child identity or preserve the unavailable status; do not acquire global history just to monitor work. Treat unexpected outside content as an exposure event: preserve the pre-exposure product and timing, do not clean its record into an unexposed result, and qualify or rerun the affected comparison through corrected means. This is an experimental exposure rule, not a ban on appropriately authorized global coordination in ordinary work.

After the return, reopen the containing question. A successful local run makes scoped means available; it does not establish universal alignment or close a larger commissioned artifact. If the next useful work is a broader construction, carry the current generative relations forward. If a failure remains, refine upstream and re-exercise. Keep measured custody, actual source use, separate-model judgments, process improvement and final product completion distinct.
