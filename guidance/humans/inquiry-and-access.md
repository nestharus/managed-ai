# What inquiry makes available

A model can interpret available material, develop projections and construct explanations, methods and artifacts. Software can retain expressed relationships and execute established operations. Together they can begin useful inquiry while the model's private computation remains inaccessible. A public account is material to examine, rather than a transcript of that computation.

## Keep consideration available without closing discovery

Reading several sources together can reveal a relation none expressed alone. A shared account may support several interests while omitting one source's important qualification. Retaining what was actually discussed helps the next activity decide where further reading would matter.

The shared engine's optional Attention support records nominated questions and public accounts, reports omissions within a selected view, and can return the wider opportunity field. The interpreter supplies new questions, adequacy judgments and the resulting work. A question can receive substantial consideration and remain open; a new relationship can become consequential after every previously nominated item has been discussed.

## Follow the result into the question it was meant to answer

A program may correctly check a supplied condition. If the research question was how to develop that condition in an unfamiliar situation, the check leaves that contribution open.

Ask what the selected work makes available that is not already a premise, and which later construction could use it. A proof, direct construction, source or encounter can provide the missing relation. A person's framing correction is also actual participation; its contribution should remain visible in the account.

## Learn from the account of the work

G60's retained research includes exact operations, source investigations and corrections supplied by its author. Accurate written guidance did not by itself establish that subsequent research followed it. A retrospective audit can expose that gap and change the current producing instructions. The consequential return is then what later work actually does through those instructions.

Different recipients may need different accounts of the same work. Preserve their useful common source while carrying the distinctions that affect each continuation.
