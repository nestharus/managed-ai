## A source request can change with the output

**Recorded analysis.** Comparing two five-year means of published global atmospheric CO2 annual growth supplies a descriptive difference. Under explicit marginal-standard-deviation assumptions, the sum of weighted scales gives a conditional envelope for its uncertainty despite unknown dependence.

That envelope does not supply the missing actual aggregate uncertainty. A receiver needing the latter still needs warranted joint participation. It need not receive the full covariance matrix: a source holding the necessary information could return the justified variance of the requested contrast. The output and producing relationship determine the missing contribution.

## A new question can reuse a developed capability

**Recorded local construction.** A running host began with two text operations. A separate model then adapted an acquired annual-growth calculation, and the host registered the reviewed code. Its first report compared two five-year means. A fresh recipient selected a shorter comparison, received the actual result from the same operation and wrote a briefing.

| Retained comparison | Earlier mean | Later mean | Difference |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2021–2025 versus 2016–2020 | 2.436 | 2.626 | +0.190 |
| 2024–2025 versus 2022–2023 | 2.465 | 2.910 | +0.445 |

All values are ppm/year. The briefing placed the higher final two-year mean beside the annual fall from 3.76 in 2024 to 2.06 in 2025. Both statements describe the same retained observations. The additional comparison helped explain the pattern without providing independent evidence or aggregate uncertainty.

The question and explanation developed while the calculation stayed adequate. Source fit concerned the retained quantity, years and uncertainty limits. Goal fit concerned whether another average helped the briefing. The model supplied that judgment; the executable supplied the requested calculation.

Both reports entered the unchanged artifact owner in the recorded occurrence. Its replay exercised supplied code and requests; it did not repeat original model authoring. Current executable availability is selected through the [shared implementation](implementation.md). A direct calculation can also complete a one-off report. ## Moving expertise into a shared producer

**Recorded local construction.** A maintainer derived four claim-and-evidence questions from a fixed version of [Code Review Workflows](https://github.com/nestharus/code-review-workflows/blob/ab3fa56d0d821e860462dc09167b92b21b883cde/risk-axis-reviewers/families/claim-and-evidence-validity.prompt.md): whether evidence can be produced, which implementation it describes, what alternatives it rules out, and which conditions it covers. The unchanged received `SharedCore` combined this expertise with the current G60 method, an actual task and selected sources. A local file broker and the maintainer's separate model launch supplied the connection. The model returned a substantive review.

The receiver used the review to reassess an earlier demonstration whose replies were explicitly authored. Its program copies complete contexts, while its checks observe narrower facts. A historical record outside the reviewer's first view contained the complete original context. That earlier omission was from the selected view, not from the archive; the record still did not show the interpreter's input or use. The actual review and expanded evidence then became inputs to a teaching proposal; the maintainer retained acceptance and publication authority.

**Illustrative consolidation duty.** If a team commissions that expertise to become part of one shared producing system, a good direct review completes only part of its work. Someone must connect the new producer to real callers and receivers, preserve required coverage and pending results, and transfer responsibilities before retiring displaced entry points. Existing useful operations can remain.

The local construction supplied real model participation and a subsequent receiving use. It did not install Cognition or complete that broader migration. What counts as finished depends on the commissioned outcome; neither arrangement becomes a universal rule.

## Construct the interface that later work uses

**Primary mechanism and separate recorded composition.** Pierce and Kuipers's sensorimotor study shows a provisional movement helping obtain the response relation used to add feedback. Terminating behaviors then offer a discrete interface. Its learning machinery, objective and physical assumptions are supplied; G60 inspected the paper rather than rerunning its simulated robots. [Constructed-interface source](reference-notes.md#constructed-interfaces).

A later G60 construction asks what makes such an interface usable by an actual continuation. Its authored finite source has two possible starting states and one forbidden state:

| Source state | Upper observation | Raw action reaching the goal | Raw action entering the forbidden state |
|---|---|---|---|
| `left` | `work` | `pulse_plus` | `pulse_minus` |
| `right` | `work` | `pulse_minus` | `pulse_plus` |

Every other raw command safe from both starts, including `hold`, leaves them unchanged. Each state has a successful raw action, but the upper observation cannot distinguish which one to choose. Repeating the safe action does not resolve that ambiguity.

The construction supplies a local feedback body with a finer observation. It reads which pulse is needed, enacts that pulse and exposes the common command `finish`. At that boundary, concrete and abstract systems use the same command token. The body supplies the action's implementation rather than merely pairing two names.

The existing symbolic policy builder and live consumer were used unchanged. Runs from both starts completed with one `finish` command and **zero upper readings**. Finer local sensing still selected the actual raw effect. The local sensor meanings and source transition laws were supplied; no learned robot or approximate source model was qualified by these runs.

The selected duty also prohibited visiting the forbidden state. An alternate macro can reach the right endpoint through that state and still violate the duty. Endpoint correspondence therefore needs the actual path guarantee when safety concerns intermediate effects. Macro termination and elapsed-time bounds likewise require their own warranted participation.

A reusable interface for a family of controllers can have stronger requirements than this selected no-reading policy. A changed possible coarse return can defeat the full relation while `finish` still has a direct completion proof. The [feedback-refinement source and local scope](reference-notes.md#implementable-control-correspondence) distinguish those claims.

The broader teaching implication is that constructing a boundary can change who needs which information. It can make later work possible while retaining finer sensing and responsibility locally. Giving the upper controller that finer observation would be another adequate arrangement; these executions do not compare their complete costs.

## A connected construction already executed

**Recorded execution.** A fixed array of 287,825 values was supplied through thirty logical writes. Source inspection and an observation of actual storage effects exposed six shared physical units. A changed writer assembled contributions by those units and reduced production to six writes, with one old-state read at the final unit.

That last unit had fewer visible positions than its nominal physical extent. All requested values were present, but the codec still needed a complete nominal input. A further producer supplied it, filling only positions outside the declared visible array, then encoded and replaced the units. The ordinary reader verified the intended values and metadata. Production now recorded six writes and zero data reads.

The construction assumed fixed shape, complete contributions and exclusive access. The model supplied the interpretation and changing producers; the tools supplied exact operations and actual returns. The possibility of the final residual was already mentioned before execution.

The useful unit changed twice: from logical region to shared storage object, then from visible extent to complete codec input. This changed subsequent production. Setup, source assembly, verification, buffering and model work remain costs in any comparison of the complete arrangements.
