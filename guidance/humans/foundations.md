# Foundations

## What is being constructed

An artifact is a contribution that can enter further work: a sentence, drawing, operation, observation procedure, theory or process. A partial construction can help discover what it is becoming.

Interpretation makes relationships consequential in a use. An established operation can execute exactly where its meaning is supplied. The applied arrangement openly uses an existing model's competence to develop those meanings, questions and connections.

A workflow is one possible producing artifact; an agent is one possible arrangement of responsibility and execution. One context can work across many observational dimensions. Distinct authority, exposure or accountability can require separate participants. The useful organization follows these relationships.

## Settled work and constructed capabilities

A constructed space retains meanings, source relationships, operations and receiving uses that already support a family of work. Some relationships can remain settled while another develops. An adequate known operation can run directly within a larger inquiry.

What a later operation treats as a starting capacity can itself be constructed. A provisional behavior may make a missing observation available; that observation may support its successor. The [interface example](worked-examples.md#construct-the-interface-that-later-work-uses) follows this possibility into an actual consumer.

Its control construction also changes the division of work. A local body uses a finer observation to implement one command for a coarser upper controller. The selected upper policy requires no readings, while the local sensing remains operative. The boundary has changed where the information is consumed. The implemented body, permitted effects and receiving duty give the correspondence its useful meaning.

Ask which relationship remains missing and where it will be consumed. An existing source may supply it directly. Another producer may remove its need. Keep independently owed explanations or source effects even when a smaller interface fulfills one selected use. Retaining a useful generator or recovery route can be more valuable than keeping every intermediate.

## Prediction and development

A prediction projects what an interpreted relationship makes possible and what follows from using it. This can expose a missing relation, revise a former antecedent or develop another operation. Existing material can acquire a new role even when the earlier prediction was correct.

The adequate floor is the contribution actually owed. An apparent ceiling is a limit of current meanings, composition or means. Develop a stronger contribution while preserving or legitimately amending the duty. A changed evaluator cannot retroactively satisfy a failed obligation.

Work forward into consequences and backward into the relationships they need. These movements can revise each other. Follow a strong connected hypothesis while leaving its missing mechanisms explicit. A proof, capable source, direct construction or encounter can supply the next relation; its result reopens the containing question.

## Whole-use composition

Placement, timing, omission and shared context change what identical ingredients do. Accurate captions under an unsupported heading can make a misleading exhibition. Repair can change the heading or activity while preserving the captions. The perspective that exposes a problem need not contain its repair.

A dimension makes a consequential distinction available. Its observing arrangement may be a view, instrument, juxtaposition or worked example. Develop and use the arrangement where its distinction matters. A name alone does not recreate its capability.

The receiving duty also sets the relevant extent of an effect. An operation that reaches the correct endpoint can pass through a forbidden intermediate state. A finite sequence of commands can still lack a time bound if their durations are unbounded. Preserve the path, timing or termination relationship where the actual task consumes it.

## Memory and re-entry

Memory can retain a result, producer, contrast, unfinished question or source route. Reuse can reconstruct another adequate realization. A duty concerning a historical occurrence instead needs evidence of that occurrence.

A useful part need not have been stored as an object: an operation can construct it from retained relationships. There are also limits to a chosen encoding. For supplied encoding `k` and answer `c`, if `k(x) = k(x')` but `c(x) != c(x')`, no function of `k` alone supplies the correct answer on both inputs. Another source or interaction can supply a different input. This criterion concerns a declared answer under established meanings; it does not require retaining every detail for every possible future.

A book's contents page illustrates the positive possibility. New work may join a preparation from one chapter, a dependency from another and a qualification from a third. That relationship can become a useful unit the book never named. Another reader may organize the same material around an explanation, operation or historical claim. The system can retain several such organizations and their source routes.

Access can change with the current use. A missing promised source contribution and a completed old review facing a stronger new question both resemble “work still pending.” The first still owes its original contribution; the second needs a new use without rewriting the old task. A retained contrast can help reconstruct that difference. [Source and scope](reference-notes.md#access-time-cues-and-useful-differences).

An encounter may also change the useful role. An exhibition's packing body could become its display support. A fit mock-up and manufacturing input may need different properties while serving one product. These are authored possibilities: develop the actual transport, physical and subsequent-use duties where the selected construction needs them. [Roles](reference-notes.md#encounters-and-new-productive-roles) · [Representations](reference-notes.md#productive-correspondence-across-representations).

Choose retention alongside the availability and cost of reconstruction. A short final representation does not price the work needed to create, interpret and maintain it.

## Reconstructing the effective observer

What counts as a piece can depend on how material is observed. A visible region or paragraph boundary can serve one use while hiding a relationship another contribution needs. The observing operation can therefore become the artifact being developed.

An accessible coupling can change observation while a constituent remains fixed. An intensity detector can report identical brightness for fields with different relative phase; introducing a coherent reference lets that difference affect brightness. The changed interaction supplies another relation. This is an explanatory use of established optics and active perception. [Sources](reference-notes.md#accessible-observer-reconstruction).

A reference can develop through the work. Readings sharing an unknown offset can support differences without first identifying it; an absolute claim needs another justified relation. That authored mathematical contrast illustrates how a useful operation can have smaller requirements than a stronger claim. [Constructed references](reference-notes.md#constructed-reference-relations).

How an output is produced can change what it reveals. Suppose a producer retries a candidate until it is accepted, then delivers only that accepted identity. Separate runs devoted to candidates A and B reveal neither candidate's chance of acceptance per attempt. Combining those finished reports adds no missing rate. If each attempt instead independently chooses A or B with equal probability, their shares among accepted outputs reveal their relative acceptance rates. Both rates can still be multiplied by the same factor without changing those shares.

A different sequence can reveal an absolute rate through the same output format: try A once; if rejected, try only B until it is accepted. The final identity is A exactly when the first attempt succeeds. Its probability is therefore A's acceptance probability. The producer already sees the rejection and remembers the switch; the changed sequence carries that event to the recipient through a later success.

This construction assumes fixed positive acceptance probabilities, independent attempts and distinguishable final identities. One returned identity is one outcome, not an exact probability. Positive fallback acceptance gives eventual completion with probability one; positivity alone supplies no fixed time bound. Retaining only runs that finish before a cutoff can change the observed distribution. An adequate direct source answer may avoid this construction. [Source and scope](reference-notes.md#producing-informative-observations).

Carry the changed observing arrangement into the continuation that needs it. A description beside the unchanged observer leaves that productive connection open. The [recorded storage example](worked-examples.md#a-connected-construction-already-executed) shows how successive observations changed the unit through which a producer acted.

## Reading and changing

A view sufficient to answer a question may not support an adequate change to its containing work. An edit must deliver the intended effect and preserve the other obligations that apply. Source support, permission and the current intended effect are separate relationships.

A source version, its presentation, an interpretation and an observed result can require different evidence. Exact copying can establish a local fidelity claim while surrounding context changes the interpretation. Likewise, determining that an operation is permitted does not supply every operand needed to run it. Follow the result into its consumer.

## An artifact and its account

An account can predict what an artifact will enable. Reading or using the artifact can return consequences that revise its account. The artifact need not disclose every private intention or producing history to support useful work. A retained description becomes consequential through what a recipient can do with it.

Human teaching and agent guidance are independently useful artifacts. Each carries the relationships its recipient needs; neither requires the whole development conversation. Their explanations can differ while remaining faithful to the same source and duties.

Internal and external are relative to an activity. A fictional scene can supply a return when its author reads what its rules and events make possible. Self-authorship does not make that return independent corroboration. The exposure and receiving context belong to its interpretation.

Several creators can meet through one representation while retaining different interests. A recurring mismatch can call for changing subsequent production as well as the current text. [Reciprocal construction](reciprocal-construction.md) develops how those contacts can remain useful.

## Acting and learning

Act on supported interpretations within actual authority. Obtain missing participation where the contribution needs it. A complete ontology, universal evaluator or fixed observer roster is not a prerequisite for every useful construction.

Keep the actual meaning of a return. Acknowledgment does not grant permission. A late result can be useful without fulfilling its original request. A changed reader or producer can enable further work while original duties remain visible.

For improvement, develop the means of a stronger contribution and compare the relevant complete arrangements. Include preparation, interpretation, access, execution, reception, repair and upkeep where they affect the decision. Counts of files, calls or tests establish neither general capability nor economy.
