# Working meanings

These are reading aids for this pack. A particular use supplies the representation, evidence and operations through which they become concrete.

**Artifact** is material that enters work: an explanation, drawing, operation, question or partial construction. The process, observer and receiver can themselves be artifacts. **Process** is a producing arrangement; a fixed sequence is one possible form.

**Interpretation** makes relationships consequential in a use. **Interest** makes a contribution matter. Several interpretations can remain useful for different interests. Their **intersection** is the co-use that needs a correspondence: a shared source, translation, property, resource or effect.

**Geometry** names interpreted relational organization and its productive possibilities, without prescribing one graph, vector or metric form. **Information** includes newly available relationships as well as uncertainty reduction. A broad use of **distribution** does not by itself specify a probability law over fixed categories.

**Projection** develops what a relationship could enable and what that use would require. A **hypothesis** proposes a relationship to develop. **Observation** receives material through an interpreted operation. **Recognition** makes a particular distinction operative. A **dimension** is such a consequential distinction; its observing means may have to be constructed.

**Constructed space** is an arrangement of meanings, sources, operations and receiving relationships that supports a family of uses. **Interface** supplies what another operation consumes. An implemented local body can retain a distinction and expose a simpler operation to its receiver. A **domain pack** makes selected competence reusable.

**Usefulness** concerns the contribution, what it enables and its economy. Potential and density are interpreted candidate instruments. **Convergence** needs a specified comparison, such as common action, local fit or a mathematical limit; different forecasts can support the same action.

**Memory** includes retention, access and reconstruction. A **cue** brings a possible use into reach. **Session compaction** is the provider's native operation, separate from external memory and reorientation. Exact occurrence evidence serves a different duty from reconstructing another adequate result.

**Goal** or question expresses an intended contribution. Actual user duties and authority remain operative when a proposal changes. **Attractor** and **bastion** are historical terms for a developing pull and a scoped arrangement of inquiry; they do not name required components.

**Semantic execution** is the capable model's fallible interpretation. Exact software operates under established meanings. **Qualification** develops or assesses a contribution under its conditions; **review** can participate in construction. Independent acceptance is a separate responsibility where required.

**Closure** is coverage by an available generating relationship under stated conditions. **Progress** changes what can be produced or used. A budget stop, failed search or larger count of documents does not supply that relationship.
