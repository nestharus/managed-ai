# Constructive methods

Choose a move for the relationship the current work needs. [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) owns selection and completion; the [guide](guide.md) defines the concepts. These moves can combine without forming a compulsory sequence.

## Locate the open relationship

Follow the intended contribution into its receiving use. Identify what is needed and what already supplies it. A known calculation can run while interpretation develops; a fixed rule can remain settled while its applicability is examined.

Compare constructing missing participation, obtaining it from an adequate source and changing the producer so it no longer needs it. Keep supplied meanings explicit. A named input feature is a starting capacity for the calculation, not a discovery made by it.

For improvement, project a positive contribution beyond the existing floor and develop a useful comparison. The [exhibition example](worked-discovery.md) begins with already accurate captions. If neither an unmet duty nor a consequential improvement is selected, retain the result and finish.

## Compose the whole

Build a draft, assembled view or enactment that exposes the arrangement's consequences. Placement, timing, repetition and shared context can alter adequate ingredients. Two successful changes may invalidate one another's conditions.

Follow an effect through its producer. Repair can change an ingredient, its placement, a receiving operation or the process. Determine what each intermediate must enable and preserve: a fit mock-up and a manufacturing input can need different properties; reaching an endpoint can still violate a path duty. [Concrete cases](worked-examples.md).

## Develop observing means

Begin with a distinction that would change the work. A view, comparison, source-side transformation or instrument may make it available. Use a concrete contrast and refine its definition through the return. Retain enough material and procedure to reconstruct that capacity.

When that distinction is not yet intelligible, exploratory composition can develop the material from which a useful observation or interpretation emerges. The method permits this development before a fixed signal is available. Keep supplied capacities explicit: a proof that assumes the missing source-to-action bridge qualifies its consequences but has not built that bridge.

Where an internal mechanism is inaccessible, work through available interactions. Determine what the reading supports: differences may be available before an absolute reference is established. Use an adequate existing observation directly. The detecting operation and its repair can be different.

When a new question outruns a retained observation, construct or obtain consequential source alternatives compatible with that observation. Does the required quantity differ between them? If it does, compare an adequate source answer with changing the interaction, producer, observing means or permitted question. If it does not, the existing observation may already suffice. This is a useful move for an unresolved relation, not a prerequisite to every direct operation.

Keep the particular repair attached to what it identifies. Mixing candidates before a common acceptance event can reveal a relative rate that mixing finished reports does not. A producer that tries a target once, then switches to a distinct fallback after rejection, can instead make the terminal identity reveal the first attempt's outcome. That route needs rejection feedback and branch memory at the producer, distinguishable outputs and adequate continuation under the source law. It identifies a probability through the terminal distribution, not one returned identity. Almost-sure completion supplies no fixed deadline; selecting only runs completed before a cutoff needs its own interpretation.

## Share work across interests

Prepare material several continuations can use, while retaining differences that affect their decisions. Counts and frequencies cannot always be replaced by a list of distinct names. Joint reading can produce separate actions; separately prepared material can enter one producer.

Record the member relationships actually addressed. A joint consequence may become important after each ingredient has been considered. [Attention support](attention.md) is optional; a common account alone does not establish complete coverage.

## Reuse and receive

Read earlier work through the current question. Recover its originating context when the present claim needs it. A completed result can serve a stronger use without reopening its original duty; unfinished work still owes its actual contribution.

A late return can change the activity or remove a request. Preserve the initial forecast, receive the actual result and connect the useful part to its next consumer through an input, procedure or product. A description beside an unchanged operation does not perform that passage.

Keep the result, generator, contrast or recovery route that later use needs. Consolidate current instructions into one operative entrance and retain particular historical evidence only for a current use or duty. Remove an intermediate after locating how its still-required contribution is supplied.

The shared engine's `recover TURN --text` retrieves the validated original producing context when needed. Reply and artifact selection do not automatically include it. Historical context may need current sources for another question. [Receiving example](worked-examples.md#receive-a-late-return-under-a-changed-request).

## Coordinate contributors

Locate the shared artifact, resource, duty or effect. Construct the correspondence needed through the participants' actual interfaces. Identify who has the relevant information and who can authorize and enact the effect. [A local body can expose an adequate upper command](worked-examples.md#make-a-command-implementable).

Clarify apparent disagreement through the occurrence, quantity, conditions and intended use. Different interests can expose different consequences; incompatible claims about one specified outcome need resolution. Check the whole arrangement where pairwise agreements rely on different conditions.

A rendered-view change and a source-state change require different participation. Obtain an actual response where another participant's contribution matters. When future production is part of the duty, repair its producing instruction as well as the current surface.

## Improve the producing arrangement

Consider another unit of work, representation, observer or producer. A removed requirement may also remove source visits, classification and upkeep. Preserve independently owed effects and future uses; equal present outputs can hide different later capabilities.

Construct complementary changes together when their effects depend on one another, then receive the arrangement in use. Compare full relevant burden, including relocated work. Retain an improvement at the scope actually supported.

A reusable operation is useful when its interpreted family serves later work. A new question may reuse it; a new source meaning may require adaptation. Acquisition, admission and useful application are separate judgments, not universal stages. [Changing capability during use](worked-examples.md#add-a-capability-and-receive-its-use) makes that distinction concrete.
