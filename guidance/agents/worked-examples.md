## Review an unfamiliar artifact

Use this entrance when a review needs help connecting its intended contribution to the arrangement that actually produces it. It applies the [operating method](AGENTS.md) with ordinary sources and files. An understood review can proceed directly; the commission below is editable support, not a required sequence or record format.

### Commission the actual decision

State what the recipient needs to decide before letting an existing product's pages, components or workflows determine the review's units. Those structures expose the implementation, but the important relationship may cross them or remove an assumed step. Preserve the user's stated purpose separately from your interpretation.

```text
Artifact and version: [the actual body, source revision or observed state]
Contribution and decision: [what useful outcome it should enable, for whom,
and what the recipient must decide from this review]
Sources and authority: [available evidence and access; permitted inspection,
experimentation and changes; particular unavailable participation]
Continuing duties: [useful direct work, accepted artifacts, pending effects
and later uses that a change must preserve]

Return a source-bound keep, repair or reorganize decision. Explain the
consequential relationship, compare an adequate direct route, and identify
what observation could change the choice. Locate any accepted correction's
responsible producer or receiver. Distinguish a source-level decision from
implementation or receiving effects the commission additionally requires.
```

These details can remain prose and develop during inspection. Missing information is a reason to qualify the affected conclusion or obtain the particular return it needs, rather than invent the answer or suspend all other useful work.

### Relate the contribution to its actual realization

Project the useful outcome forward and its necessary participation backward. Inspect how sources become inputs, how producers transform them, who can make a result effective, and what later work receives. Compare the current arrangement with a concrete alternative. A local defect may call for a local repair; another representation, source or producer may remove the problematic work while preserving its real purpose.

Give each consequential relation its own meaning and support. In prose or a diagram, name what the edge asserts: reading, producing, selecting, invalidating, authorizing or adopting. Bind that claim to the relevant source passage and version or observed occurrence. A file citation alone cannot establish every relationship drawn beside it. A read does not establish a write; a wakeup does not establish publication; an available capability does not establish that a consumer selects it.

Keep source-reachable behavior, recorded execution and useful receiving outcomes distinct when the decision depends on them. Interpret quantities, identities and conditions at the receiving boundary: compatible field names do not establish compatible meaning. A missing recorded edge does not establish that no consequential relationship exists. Conversely, inspect known adequate paths before proposing new discovery or coordination machinery.

Develop an acceptance observation that could distinguish the promised contribution from a convenient weaker result. Try to construct a realization that passes the proposed check while leaving the important limitation intact. Carry that case through earlier effects and later consumers; the perspective detecting a failure need not own its repair.

### A delayed proposal can pass the wrong acceptance check

**Authored conditional construction.** An owner retains immutable artifact revisions and selects one as current. A contributor reads A0 and prepares proposal P. Direct work may accept a new revision while that contribution is pending; unrelated artifacts may also continue changing. A receiving view can reject a proposal, while a fresh consumer obtains whichever revision the owner currently selects.

The promised duty is to consider useful proposals without silently displacing later accepted work. A reviewer recommends checking the proposal's basis in the receiving view. Its proposed success observation is “the view rejects the obsolete proposal.” Consider this permitted sequence in an implementation that fails the duty:

| Event | Consequence |
|---|---|
| The contributor starts from A0. | P's producing basis is A0. |
| Direct work accepts A1; the view reads it. | The owner selects A1 and the view holds A1. |
| The contributor stores P and selects it as current before notification. | The owner selects P; A1 can still exist in history. |
| The view compares P with A1 and rejects it. | The proposed check passes but does not undo the earlier selection. |
| A fresh consumer requests the current artifact. | It receives P. |

No revision bytes need be destroyed for the promise to fail. Retention and the view's correct rejection do not preserve which revision later work actually receives.

Two sufficient repairs follow under these assumptions. The producer can retain P as a candidate without changing the current selection. Alternatively, an eligible accepting operation can check the actual target and producing basis in the same indivisible transition that selects the replacement. Expecting A0 then cannot replace current A1. A check followed by a separate unconditional write leaves the intervening-change problem intact. Capture the basis when production reads its input; a newly stamped completion time cannot supply that relationship.

A justified merge or rebase can permit more useful work than exact-basis matching. Its adequacy needs the actual contribution's meaning. Apply the selected protection at every operation that can make a result authoritative for this use, including earlier writers and other receivers.

Acceptance must also preserve useful continuation. A current-basis contribution can still be accepted, direct edits and unrelated work can proceed, and an obsolete candidate can remain available for comparison. Rejecting every contribution or freezing permitted activity does not fulfill this promise. If meaningful content consideration is also owed, inspect the content and its reason. Under a supplied rule such as “include an item exactly when its marker is present,” a permanently pending receipt does not complete a decidable inclusion case. Genuine unresolved meaning can instead warrant a retained question.

This construction supplies a counterexample and repairs within stated assumptions. It reports no deployed incident or measured reviewing benefit. Another artifact supplies its own authority, acceptance meaning and useful concurrent activity; those can change the remedy.

### Carry the decision into use

A justified source-level decision can complete the review. When the commission also owes a change, follow the decision into its responsible instruction, operation or owner. Check the actual selected input or installed behavior and the next consumer's result. Updating a recommendation beside an unchanged producer does not perform that passage.

Preserve accepted work and pending effects while the new arrangement becomes usable. Retire a displaced path only after locating the functioning home of its still-required contribution. An adequate direct path can remain. Report the completed contribution, exact support, adopted correction and particular remaining return. A missing installation or independent evaluation limits claims requiring it; it does not reopen a completed source decision.

## Make a command implementable

**Recorded finite construction with supplied meanings.** A symbolic continuation must reach a goal without entering a forbidden state. Its source has two admitted starts with the same upper observation:

| `left` | `work` | `pulse_plus` | Enters the forbidden state |

Concrete and abstract interfaces use that same token at the body's boundary. The local body gives the command its effect; a state-name correspondence alone would not implement it.

## Reconstruct a part

**Recorded execution.** Three component triangles were represented by nine segments. Collinear normalization retained six maximal segments. A supplied geometric recognizer constructed five supported triangular boundaries, including a central boundary absent from the original component records. That boundary became the operand of a transformation that changed the inside while preserving the outer silhouette.

The retained linework supported a newly useful part through a different operation. Recovering the original component grouping would be a separate historical question. Retain the recognizer and its geometric conditions when carrying this construction into another use.

The intended effect then raises another question: **where should the change enter subsequent production?** For a union of source linework, two duties select different operations:

| Duty | Producing route |
|---|---|
| Change contributing source states | Address every current supplier of the removed support, provide the addition through an admitted source, and preserve unrelated source material. Obtain the corresponding support and authority. |
| Preserve a fixed effect in the rendered view | Apply the retained removal and addition to the aggregate after each relevant rebuild. |

For the view route, let `U` be the supplied aggregate, `D` the fixed removal, and `A` the addition:

`F(U) = (U minus D) union A`.

The executed consumer requires an aggregate on every call and stores no aggregate default. It preserves supplied linework outside the declared effect and leaves source states unchanged. Upstream compilation still obtains the source contributions. The caller supplies the aggregate appropriate to the occurrence; a label or hash binds that input without establishing its currency or completeness. A changed intended part requires an appropriate new interpretation of the rule.

The source-editing record rebuilt one edited snapshot twice, establishing its connection to the compositor. The view rule was also exercised on rebuilt archived snapshots. When the task includes a creator's next contribution, obtain that contribution or an adequate account of its producing relationship, then carry the effect into that operation.

## Let a contribution acquire another role

**Authored illustration.** A travelling exhibition needs an item protected in transit and supported on display. Looking only for a separate stand hides another possibility: the packing body might become the plinth after unpacking.

The useful construction is the arrangement that can serve both roles. Develop its support, handling, finish, access, permissions, and any return-shipping duty where those affect the use. A suitable body would remove the need for a separate resource while preserving the original transport contribution.

Now consider what a mock-up must supply. One representation may expose exterior fit in the exhibition space, while another becomes an input that a manufacturing process transforms. Their required correspondence follows their uses; the manufacturing precursor may deliberately differ from the final shape.

Receive each artifact through the operation that consumes it. A fit demonstration can guide the design while production still needs another input. The combination can reconstruct the artifact and the arrangement of work, making one existing resource useful in a role that the original search never named.
