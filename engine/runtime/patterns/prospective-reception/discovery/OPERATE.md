# Working from a large list

Start with the contribution someone needs and the circumstance in which they will receive it. What might the proposed whole enable them to do, or wrongly lead them to believe? Consider an adequate direct route. If it already serves that use, act directly. A developing consequence can change what is worth exploring next. In the research this pull is sometimes called an *attractor*. Its strength is an interpretation in context.

**Hold the prediction before seeking the answer.** Keep enough of the candidate arrangement to expose a difference. It can be a sentence, a sketch, a decision with two possible outcomes, code that can be tried, or a partial in another medium. From the predicted effect work backward to a relationship the candidate needs. Work backward again to an observation, authority or capability capable of distinguishing that relationship. Any step can change the intended whole or the means used to build it. A known route may allow several steps to be taken together.

Use the list as a set of possible lenses. A synopsis, an existing domain map, or a few targeted nominations can each be the right starting view. Retrieve a dimension’s operative source and qualifiers when that distinction can change a pending decision. Search rank and capped delivery leave other candidates unexamined. Keep a register of any scope the commission makes mandatory even when its contents are outside the current turn. Keep the cost of reading the catalogue separate from the cost of preparing its index.

**Construct what will expose the joint effect.** A single interim artifact may bring several dimensions to bear. A single dimension can call for several incompatible constructions, or for no new artifact. Try the partial or expose it to a receiver with an appropriate task. Save enough for another participant to recover the prediction, the scoped version and authority, the observation, the rival it discriminated, and what happened when the result reached its next use. A saved investigation can change the underlying source interpretation, another passage, the placement of the partial, the measuring instrument or the workflow. Further inquiry follows the resulting gap. A list of newly named candidates is a place to choose from; a promise to construct them all requires a separate commission.

**Use the saved work at its actual scope.** Check whether the source, assumptions, arrangement and receiver that mattered to the investigation still apply. Reuse the evidence body once; explain separately how each receiving decision depends on it. Test a changed relationship in its new context. Split work where observations can proceed independently or need another authority, then inspect the interaction after the returns meet. Let the interpreter make that split. A repeated keyword, common category or absence of a shared tag cannot decide it.

Building a domain is itself a task. When a distinction recurs, retain a recoverable observation or instrument, together with a contrast, an adequate rival and the conditions that reopen it. Record a possible overlap as a hypothesis. One operation that actually discriminates two separately stated consequences supplies scoped shared evidence. Statistical correlation is a different claim: define the variables and unit, collect joint measurements, retain missingness and plausible common causes, and check the relationship on other cases before depending on it. An observed association alone does not establish that one finding can replace another. At large scale, begin with the active uses and saved investigations; test likely neighbors rather than enumerating every pair. Keep exceptions discoverable.
