Keep three duties distinct: source-to-clause entailment, integrity of the exact join, and adequacy of the containing account. The participant supplies the first and third judgments; the selected composer checks its stated byte contract. A false initial interpretation can pass all mechanical checks. Accurate clauses can form an incomplete or misleading selection.
