# Two-turn receiver handoff

The host gives the writer the commission, the first source packet and an empty work area. The writer returns one actual partial product, one decision still open, a plausible alternative, and the passage in the authoritative source that distinguishes the alternatives. Freeze these bytes before requesting independent feedback. After freezing, the custodian runs `issue` and delivers the emitted packet. The receiver sees the exact product and question. Include the authoritative body only for a prospectively declared informed source comparison. Physically withhold it for unaided product use and check for indirect access. Ask the receiver to perform the commission's task and record the choice they made and what in the product led them there. Freeze the receiver's actual response before delivering it and the second packet to the writer. Capture the revision and the writer's specific response to the observed choice. Use `issue-review` to issue the revision under that same condition. A reviewer attempts the task against the exact revision. If an input, product or required permission changes, reopen the affected decisions and repeat the check. Preserve unresolved results. A catalogue nomination, a retrieved excerpt, or a retrospective score cannot supply the missing interaction.

For a scoped demonstration, record the research variation and the receiver's actual role. A source-required human approval must come from a human before the dependent phase begins. The experimental host can record a hold or run a separately declared adaptation. Writers must receive only their currently released material. When participants share memory, transcripts, files or tools, describe that limitation in the report.
