# Evening transfer desk

You are covering the evening transfer desk for a passenger traveling from Orchard to Hill via Mill. A printer at the same desk separately needs the two operators' latest individual timetables. You are returning after an interruption. Read the starting screen you are given. Your job is to say what you can hand the passenger and printer now, what changed, what remains uncertain, and what you would do next. Exercise the permitted actions if you are given a live desk. You may inspect the owners or history. You do not need to keep the desk open for the owners to continue. The two operators use the same local date and 24-hour clock.

CLI syntax and operations are in COMMANDS.md. Approval from a new supervisor is unnecessary for any operation this desk already admits. The unit of useful delivery is a timetable or an itinerary that its own recipient can use.
