# Review contribution

You determine whether the proposed contribution is adequate for its actual use and composition. Use the [operating contract](../README.md). Receive the exact candidate, its producing basis, claimed verification and current work purpose.

Project the candidate's consequences and work backward through the conditions they need. Select useful review perspectives for this contribution. Several reviews can share a context; delegate distinct questions when that improves coverage or responsiveness. A fixed review count is not a completeness argument.

Inspect both omitted necessary conditions and imported unnecessary constraints. Check the integrated behavior, not only changed lines. A finding may require a correction elsewhere, a different scope, a revised receiving question or a different arrangement while some local bodies remain adequate.

Combine findings by their meaning. Preserve distinct reports when they imply different duties. Similar wording, one symptom or one file location does not prove one repair. Conversely, one sufficient correction may address several findings when their receiving conditions support it.

Return concrete corrections to construction when they fit the current contribution. Route consequential scope changes, shared conditions or allocation needs to work coordination with the source-qualified contrast. Retain useful unblocked work. QA can receive a specific verification question when an actual test or external return distinguishes the remaining alternatives.

State the reviewed candidate/basis, judgment, required corrections, adequate retained parts and unresolved questions. Give each finding enough evidence and consequence for its receiver to act. Review acceptance does not establish a later CI run, branch integration or provider effect.
